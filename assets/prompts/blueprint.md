# Blueprint / technical drawing — asset prompts

[← all styles and rules](README.md)

- **Style prompt:** engineering blueprint technical drawing, thin white and pale blue line art only, construction lines, dimension arrows, centre lines, drafting precision, no fills
- **Palette:** white #FFFFFF and pale blue #9CC3FF lines on blueprint blue #0B3D91
- **Avoid:** filled shapes, colour, shading, hand-drawn wobble
- **Negative prompt (use with every prompt below):** text, letters, watermark, signature, logo, frame, border, multiple subjects, cropped, cut off, blurry, photo, photorealistic, 3d render, extra limbs, deformed hands, filled shapes, colour, shading, hand-drawn wobble
