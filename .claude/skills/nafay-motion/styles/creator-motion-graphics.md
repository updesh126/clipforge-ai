# Creator Motion Graphics (Kinetic Type)

The high-energy business-creator edit: huge condensed caps slamming in word by word, a yellow serif accent on the one word that matters, highlighter swipes, hand-drawn circles and arrows, tilted browser and phone mockups, rolling green counters, film grain and light leaks on near-black.

Reference code: `creator-motion-graphics.js` (demo: "How to get real leads at events", 21.5 s). Fonts: Anton, Instrument Serif italic, Inter.

## Replace for a new film
TOPIC, POSTER, TL, CUTS, PUNCH, SHAKE, FLASH, LEAK, PUSH, the value curves, the scene functions, SCENES, the chrome() label text, scoreSound(), BOARD and ASSUME. Keep: CM/SFX (scripts/core.js), word, row, slam, swipe, slamMarked, strike, scribble, handArrow, drawYaw, bubble, background, camera, finishing and the compositor (explainer).

## The look
- Near-black edit room, slow warm and cool glows, faint drifting grid, vignette, film grain stepped at 24 fps. background() and finishing() run on every frame.
- Anton caps for statements, 80–220 px. Instrument Serif italic in yellow for the one soft phrase. Inter for labels. At most one yellow word per line; green only for money and growth numbers; red only for the wrong idea.
- Words slam in one at a time. Cards and chips pop with overshoot. Mockups swing in with drawYaw or slide up.
- Emphasis: punch zoom + shake on the hardest words, yellow swipe that turns the word dark, hand-drawn ring and arrow, red strike-through for the wrong idea, stamp for a flat no.
- Key number: rolling green counter with glow; on lock a boom, white flash, light leak and spark streaks.
- Proof shots are code-drawn mockups with invented names; illustrative figures are labelled as examples.
- Every new idea gets a cut, rotating whip, RGB glitch and flash. Inside a scene lines stack, shift, get struck, and new lines arrive.
- Chrome: small topic label top left; a STEP pill only for a real sequence.
- Pace: hook lands in under 2 s, something new every 0.5–1.5 s, takeaway holds about 1.5 s.

## Legibility at 1280×720
Statements ≥ 80 px, labels the story needs ≥ 28 px, key numbers ≥ 120 px.

## Sound
Low drone and riser into the first big word; k.music under the body; tape stop on the wrong idea; beat back for the takeaway. slam for word slams (bigger word, more gain), pop for pills/chips/cards, whoosh/glitch/slam for cuts, marker for swipes and hand marks, strike, keys, send, ping, click, tick only when a counter's shown number changes and at most 12 a second, boom + slam + shimmer on the big number with a breath of near-silence before it, stamp for a rejection. At most about 3 sounds at once.
