# Clean Tech (Kinetic Type)

A calm, bright product-explainer look: cool off-white with a drifting dot grid, crisp Inter Tight words that rise into place, white UI cards with soft shadows, one electric blue accent, mono labels and a green check for anything finished.

Reference code: `clean-tech.js` (demo: "How a help-bot answers from your own docs", 20 s). Fonts: Inter Tight 500–800, JetBrains Mono 500 and 700.

## Replace for a new film
TOPIC, POSTER, TL, CUTS, BUMPS, the counter curve, LOOP and ARCS, promptCard(), node(), the scene functions, SCENES, scoreSound(), BOARD and ASSUME. Keep: rr, track, rise, riseLine, pillBehind, typed, softCard, checkMark, composite, padBed, background(), cam() and the vignette in explainer().

## The look
- Off-white #f5f6f8, slowly drifting dot grid, faint blue glow top right and green glow bottom left, light vignette, on every frame.
- Inter Tight 800 for statements, never all caps. Inter Tight 500–700 for card text. JetBrains Mono caps with letter spacing for small labels. Ink #0d0f14, muted grey for secondary text, blue #2f5bff as the only accent, green #18b46b only for done and checked.
- Words rise from behind a mask (rise, riseLine). Cards slide up and fade in, nodes pop with a small overshoot, input text is typed with a blinking caret.
- A blue pill grows behind the one key word and the word turns white (pillBehind). Small blue glows on whatever is active. A green check pops in for anything finished.
- Ideas become small working diagrams from UI pieces: cards, mono labels, progress bars, a dashed path with a travelling dot, a counter. Invented names only.
- Gentle push-in per scene, small bump when a pill lands. Clean horizontal pushes for a new idea, zoom-through into a detail. No glitches or flashes.
- Pace: hook lands in under 2 s, one new element every 0.3–1 s, takeaway holds about 1.5 s.

## Legibility at 1280×720
Statements ≥ 78 px, card text ≥ 26 px, mono labels ≥ 18 px, key numbers ≥ 120 px.

## Sound
Soft pad (padBed) with light hats and a gentle kick under the body, out for the hook and ending. Tiny glassy tick per rising word (rise1); clean swipe with a bell for blue pills; pop for cards and nodes; keys and send; whoosh for pushes; a short riser then whoosh for a zoom-through; two-note chime for checks; tick only when a counter's shown number changes. At most about 3 sounds at once; a short gap before the takeaway pill.
