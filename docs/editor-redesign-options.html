Three ways to make the editor work like OpenFilm Studio

OpenFilm puts the AI agent in a full-height chat column beside the film. You switch between editors (Screening, Edit, Canvas, Color) over one project, and you can point at a moment, a stretch of time or a part of the frame to tell the agent what to change. The mocks below are drawn at the real panel sizes from OpenFilm's source, using a ClipForge project.

B · Studio layout in ClipForge's own colours

OpenFilm's layout, sizes and density, with ClipForge's mint as the accent on near-black. The accent only marks what is selected or current, and the main buttons are light grey ("ink"), as in OpenFilm.

What changes

  • A 32px top bar with project tabs and one editor switcher (Screening · Edit · Canvas · Color, Alt+1…4) replaces the big nav inside the editor.
  • The AI chat becomes a full-height column (420px, drag to 340–800, Ctrl+Shift+L to hide), with each step the AI takes and an "Undo all".
  • The library opens inside the chat column by default, or docks left, right or bottom.
  • The inspector gets a tab strip (Text, Motion, Properties, Adjust, Effects, Audio) instead of one long form.
  • Named tracks with colour by kind, markers, snap, and a zoom slider.

Point at anything

  • 1 a moment: press M or click the ruler.
  • 2 a stretch: drag along the ruler.
  • 3 part of the frame: drag a box on the paused picture.
  • A selected clip or caption becomes a pill too (Ctrl+L).
  • The pills travel with your message, so the AI knows exactly which clip, time and area you mean.

Why B

  • Same workflow as OpenFilm, but it still looks like ClipForge and BuzyBug (mint, pink, lavender, peach on near-black).
  • OpenFilm's teal would clash with the mint already used across the Clip, Script and Home pages.

A · Studio, matched to OpenFilm's colours too

The same layout as B with OpenFilm's own tokens: #141414 background, teal accent, Inter. It is the closest match, but the editor would then look different from the rest of ClipForge unless every page is re-coloured.

Trade-off

  • Closest to OpenFilm, down to the colours.
  • Needs the Home, Clip, Script and Library pages re-coloured as well, or the app looks like two products.

C · Keep today's layout, add Studio's features

The library stays on the left and the AI stays on the right, sharing a column with the inspector as it does now. You get the editor switcher, pointing, resizable panes and the new timeline, but not the chat-first column. It is the least relearning for people who already use ClipForge.
