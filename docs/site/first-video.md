# Your first video

This walk-through makes a 30-second story video from a script.

1. Open **Script** in the top bar.
2. Paste a short story, one scene per paragraph. Click **Use the story template** if you want an example (see the [script guide](#/docs/script-guide)).
3. Under **Look**, keep **Story**.
4. Under **Illustration**, choose **Vector** (works everywhere) or **AI painting** (needs [Stable Diffusion](#/docs/stable-diffusion)).
5. Pick an **Art style**, for example *Children's storybook* or *Warli*.
6. Choose a **Format** (9:16 for Shorts and Reels) and a **Voice**.
7. Click **Create video**. You will see each step: plan → voice → word timing → scenes → build.
8. The edit opens in the **Editor**. Play it, change anything, then click **Render MP4**.

> **Note:** the first render bundles the graphics and fetches headless Chrome once, so it is slower. Later renders are much faster.
