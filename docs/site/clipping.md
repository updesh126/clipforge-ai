# Clip long videos

**Clip** turns a podcast, talk or stream into short vertical clips.

1. Click **Select Local Video**. The file is used in place, never copied. You can also paste a file path, a YouTube link, or try the sample.
2. Wait while it transcribes and scores the video (roughly 5–15 minutes per hour of footage).
3. In the studio:
   - Clips are listed in time order, each with a **Virality** score from 0 to 100.
   - **+ Add clip** adds your own section at the playhead; the bin icon removes a clip.
   - Trim with the In and Out handles. Pick 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9, **Fill** or **Fit full video**, then zoom and pan.
   - Choose captions: Off, Clean, Bold, Karaoke or Pop.
   - **Motion & voice**: switch on a hook title and a voice intro.
4. Click **Export MP4** (or **Export all**). You get a watermark-free MP4 and an SRT file in `data/exports/`.

## Open a clip in the editor

**Open in editor** builds a full edit from a clip: footage, karaoke captions, hook banner, zoom punches, progress bar and end card. From there you can change everything.
